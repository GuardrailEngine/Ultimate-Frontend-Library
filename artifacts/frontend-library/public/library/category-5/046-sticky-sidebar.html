The handbook

Small choices, held together.

A sticky index keeps the reader oriented through a long, useful page.

PrinciplePracticeMeasure
01 / Principle

Make the system visible.

People can only make good decisions about work they can see. A clear structure turns a long page into a series of understandable moments.

“The sidebar is not an ornament. It is a promise that the reader will never be lost.”
02 / Practice

Keep the context close.

As the content changes, the navigation stays nearby. This removes the friction of remembering where a section belongs and lets attention stay with the idea.

03 / Measure

Notice what people return to.

A good information architecture gives you a useful signal: which questions are worth making easier next time.

The layout is doing its job when the reader remembers the content, not the navigation.
